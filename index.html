Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Quilt Campus collects, uses, stores, and protects information for its school community features.

Overview

Quilt Campus is a school community app that helps students and families view clubs, events, polls, schedules, posts, attendance check-ins, and rewards. This policy applies to the Quilt Campus mobile app and related pages or services.

By using Quilt Campus, you agree to the collection and use of information described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the app.

Last updated: July 24, 2026

Information We Collect

Quilt Campus collects information needed to provide account access, school membership, and app features.

Account and profile information

  • Google sign-in information, such as email address, display name, and user ID.
  • Profile information, such as account role, school ID, optional student ID, real name, display name, profile photo URL, account status, followed clubs, parent-student links, and reward points.

School and activity information

  • Club information, club follows, club posts, post attachments, comments or notes included in posts, and public or school-only post visibility settings.
  • Event information, event submissions, meeting locations entered as text, attendance or check-in records, and QR-code check-in activity.
  • Poll information, poll options, and poll votes.
  • Parent-student connection information, such as family codes, QR connection requests, relationship descriptions, request notes, and approval status.
  • Reports submitted for moderation, including the reported content, selected report reason, and any additional context provided.

Uploaded content

Users may choose to upload profile photos, club post images, or file attachments. Uploaded content is stored so it can be shown inside the app where permitted by the app's access rules.

Device permissions

Quilt Campus may request camera access for QR-code scanning. The app uses QR codes for attendance, event check-ins, and parent-student connection flows. Quilt Campus does not require GPS location access for its core features.

How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Authenticate users and maintain secure account access.
  • Show the correct school, club, event, poll, and schedule information.
  • Support role-based access for current students, parents, prospective students, and school administrators.
  • Allow students to follow clubs, create posts, vote in polls, submit events, check in to activities, and track reward points.
  • Allow parents to view linked student school activity after the student approves the connection.
  • Allow prospective students to browse public school and club information.
  • Support moderation, safety review, and administrator workflows.
  • Maintain app reliability, prevent misuse, and improve the app experience.

How We Store and Share Information

Quilt Campus uses Firebase and Google services for authentication, database storage, file storage, and related app infrastructure. Information may be stored in Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Firebase Storage.

We do not sell user personal information. We do not use app data for third-party advertising or tracking. Information is shared only as needed to operate the app, provide school-community features, comply with legal obligations, protect users, or respond to authorized school or administrator review workflows.

Some content may be visible to other users depending on the feature and visibility setting. For example, public school or club previews may be visible to prospective users, while student-only features and protected posts are limited to verified users or authorized roles.

Data Retention and Deletion

Quilt Campus keeps account, profile, school, club, event, poll, report, upload, and reward information for as long as needed to provide the app, maintain records, support moderation, and meet school or legal requirements.

Users may request deletion of their account or personal information by contacting us at hello@codingmind.com. Some information may be retained if needed for security, moderation, legal compliance, backup, or school recordkeeping purposes.

Children and Student Privacy

Quilt Campus is designed for school community use and may be used by students, parents, prospective students, and school administrators. Student-only features are intended to be limited to verified or authorized users. Parents can access view-only student activity only after a connection is approved through the app's family connection flow.

If you are a parent, guardian, student, or school representative and have questions about student information, account access, or deletion, please contact us at hello@codingmind.com.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user information, including authenticated access, Firebase security rules, role-based access controls, and moderation workflows. No system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, but we work to protect information from unauthorized access, misuse, or loss.

Your Choices

  • You can choose what optional profile information and uploaded content you provide.
  • You can decline camera access, but QR-code scanning features may not work without it.
  • You can sign out of the app at any time.
  • You can request account or data deletion by contacting us.
Contact for privacy requests: hello@codingmind.com

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Quilt Campus changes. When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after updates means you accept the revised policy.
